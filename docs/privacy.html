Horde Survival Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Horde Survival mobile game ("the app") for Android and iOS handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information. It has no user accounts.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score locally on your device so it can show it the next time you play. This value never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network use

The app works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

Third parties

Because no data is collected, none is sold or shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
